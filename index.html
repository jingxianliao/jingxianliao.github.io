Research

Aerodynamics and actuator effectiveness change as convertible aircraft transition between hover and forward flight. I address these challenges through aerodynamic prediction and adaptive control allocation.

AeroKAN

Aerodynamic modeling

A Kolmogorov–Arnold network whose edge functions combine an analytical lift prior with a learned residual to capture nonlinear aerodynamics.

Symmetric airfoils
24.8% lower MAE
MAE of 0.0246 across 57 configurations where the prior applies, compared with a no-prior KAN.
Cambered airfoils
20.8% lower MAE
Across 23 configurations from 11 airfoils, mean MAE was 0.0566 with edge encoding and 0.0715 with loss-penalty encoding.
Paper and edge-function figure

allocKAN

Adaptive control

Adapts control allocation online as actuator effectiveness changes. A composite Lyapunov analysis establishes conditions for uniformly ultimately bounded closed-loop behavior.

Compared with allocMLP
37.9 s vs 75.7 s
Mean recovery time across six abrupt-fault simulations. Validation MSE was 75.2% lower on average in matched-budget offline comparisons.
Compared with conventional allocators
Bounded closed-loop states
All five conventional allocators diverged under dual actuator reversal, both on its own and with wind disturbance and inertia uncertainty.
Paper and closed-loop architecture

Current research

Convertible aircraft

I study gray-box transition dynamics for tiltrotor aircraft by combining physical models with data. Flight validation is planned as part of the ongoing project.

Embodied intelligence

I explore compact models for robot dynamics and control, starting with budgeted KAN edge-function selection and manipulator end-effector position prediction.

Selected papers

  1. EAAI2026

    Aerodynamics-encoded Kolmogorov–Arnold networks for lift-coefficient modelling across a convertible aircraft transition envelope

    AeroEdge combines an analytical lift prior, B-splines and SiLU on every network edge. Wind-tunnel data from symmetric and cambered airfoils are used to evaluate transition lift predictions.

    Jingxian Liao, Yun Li

    Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, 186, 116648

    DOI
  2. EAAI2026

    Kolmogorov–Arnold network-based adaptive control allocation for overactuated systems with Lyapunov-stable learning

    Derives Lyapunov stability conditions for online allocation. Across three controllers, allocKAN reduced the mean final state norm by 37.6–46.6% relative to allocMLP.

    Jingxian Liao, Chenkai Cao, Yun Li

    Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, 173, 114453

    DOI
  3. Drones2023

    Transition Nonlinear Blended Aerodynamic Modeling and Anti-Harmonic Disturbance Robust Control of Fixed-Wing Tiltrotor UAV

    Models nonlinear transition aerodynamics and integrates a harmonic disturbance observer with a super-twisting sliding mode controller in the longitudinal feedback loop.

    Jingxian Liao, Hyochoong Bang
