Physics-embedded learning and adaptive control for convertible aircraft

I received my Ph.D. in aerospace engineering from KAIST. My research addresses two related challenges in the transition from hover to forward flight: predicting how aerodynamic loads change with flight conditions and maintaining closed-loop performance as actuator effectiveness varies.

I embed analytical physics priors directly in learning models and use stability analysis to guide online updates in control allocation. My current work includes gray-box modeling of tiltrotor transition dynamics and the use of compact learned models for dynamics and control in embodied systems.

Research at a glance

Learning algorithms

Physics-embedded learning

Embeds physical priors in the learning architecture and uses data to capture nonlinear behavior that is difficult to model analytically. Control theory

Adaptive control with stability analysis

Uses Lyapunov analysis to guide online learning and feedback design, and to establish conditions for closed-loop stability as system properties change. System identification

Physics–data fusion for dynamics modeling

Combines physical dynamics with experimental data to build interpretable gray-box models of system behavior across operating regimes. Ongoing research

Learning and control for embodied systems

Explores compact learned models for dynamics prediction and closed-loop control under model-size and computational constraints.
