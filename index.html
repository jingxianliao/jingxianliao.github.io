Physics-embedded learning and adaptive control

I develop machine learning methods that embed physical knowledge and adaptive control methods grounded in stability analysis. My work addresses aerodynamic modeling and online control allocation for convertible aircraft during transition, with ongoing research on modeling and control for embodied intelligence.
