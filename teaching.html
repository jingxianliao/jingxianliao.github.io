Fall semester, 2026–27 · Graduate course · 2 credits

Computational Artificial Intelligence (CAI)

The Shenzhen Institute for Advanced Study, UESTC, offers this course to master’s and doctoral students in electronics, information engineering, and related disciplines. The course comprises 16 hours of lectures, 16 hours of laboratory sessions, and 32 hours of independent study and project work.

Course topics

  • Neural networks, KANs, and reinforcement learning
  • Heuristic optimization, large language models, state-space models, and world models
  • MATLAB laboratory exercises and a short research project
