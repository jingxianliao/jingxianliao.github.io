Teaching and academic service

Fall 2026 · Graduate course · 2 credits

Computational Artificial Intelligence (CAI)

Offered by the Shenzhen Institute for Advanced Study, UESTC, for master’s and doctoral students in electronic information and related disciplines. The course includes 16 hours of lectures, 16 hours of labs, and 32 hours of independent study and project work.

Course topics

  • Neural networks, KANs, and reinforcement learning
  • Heuristic optimization, large language models, state-space models, and world models
  • MATLAB labs and a research mini-project
